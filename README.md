# My Favorite Series: Vincenzo

*Vincenzo* is a South Korean crime-comedy drama that aired on *tvN* in 2021. It has 20 episodes and stars Song Joong-ki as the lead.
## Main Characters

| Character | Played by | Role |
|-----------|-----------|------|
| Vincenzo Cassano | Song Joong-ki | Mafia lawyer who fights corruption |
| Hong Cha-young | Jeon Yeo-been | Lawyer and Vincenzo's partner |
| Jang Han-seo | Ok Taec-yeon | Babel Group's ruthless chairman |
| Choi Myung-hee | Kim Yeo-jin | Babel Group's lawyer |

## Why I Like It

- It mixes *action*, *comedy* and drama really well
- Vincenzo and Cha-young have great chemistry
- The Geumga Plaza tenants add heart and humor
- It shows good people fighting corruption in unusual ways
